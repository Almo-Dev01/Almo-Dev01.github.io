# Almo

*Independent mobile game developer.*

Welcome! I'm Almo, and I make small Android games that are easy to pick up and fun to replay.

## Ci-Trouille

A Halloween game for Android: light as many pumpkins as you can before the night ends, while avoiding the creatures on the prowl.

- Several terrains and difficulty levels
- Characters to play, achievements to unlock and a bestiary to complete
- A local leaderboard and a **world leaderboard**, under a randomly generated nickname
- No ads, no account to create
- Available in French, English, Spanish and Japanese

*Coming soon to Google Play.*
